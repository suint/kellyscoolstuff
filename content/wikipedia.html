I am a longtime Wikipedia editor and have run the user group Wikimedians of Chicago since 2024. After a period of dormancy in 2020, I decided to revive the group and regularly host meetups, including a Wikicurious event in collaboration with Wikimedia NYC and a Wikipedia Day meetup for Wikipedia's 25th anniversary with over 150 attendees (covered here by Block Club Chicago). Since its revival, the list of meetup attendees has over doubled and we have helped expand over 55 Chicago-related articles in edit-a-thons and article drives.

My Wikipedia profile page has a list of articles I've created or made major contributions to. Some interesting articles I've created or majorly contributed to include Sandy Liang, Corset, and Filipinos in Chicago. I am also proud to have expanded The Corner That Held Them to Good Article status.
